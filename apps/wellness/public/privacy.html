Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

Care Bridge 360 is the member and family app of Care Bridge Home, a home-care service in Kerala, India. The app is run by [legal entity name and registered address] ("we", "us"). Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here.

What we collect

Data Why
Account details: name, email address, password (stored hashed) To create your account and sign you in.
Member profile: name, contact and emergency-contact details, diagnoses, allergies So your care team knows who you are and how to help.
Health entries you add: check-ins, blood pressure, glucose, weight, medications and doses taken To track your health and medicines and share them with your care team.
Documents you upload: lab reports, prescriptions, scans To keep your records in one place for you and your care team.
Wearable and health-app data, only if you allow it: heart rate, blood oxygen, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate, steps, activity, sleep, walking speed and steadiness, VO2 max, wrist temperature, ECG and irregular-rhythm alerts Read from Apple Health, Android Health Connect or Fitbit (through Google) to show your trends and alert your care team to changes.
Location, only when you send an SOS alert So your care team knows where to find you in an emergency. We do not track your location at any other time.
Device information: push notification token, app crash reports To send you notifications and fix problems. Crash reports have names, contact details and health notes removed before they leave your device.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your data

  • You and the family members linked to your member record with an invite code.
  • The care coordinator and care staff assigned to you. Any on-call coordinator can see an SOS alert, so emergencies are never missed.
  • Service providers who run parts of the app for us and may use the data only to provide that service:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Mumbai, India.
    • Cloudflare: delivers the app's web content.
    • Sentry: crash reporting, with personal details removed (hosted in the EU).
    • Resend: sends account and notification emails.
    • Apple and Google: deliver push notifications; Google also provides Fitbit data if you connect Fitbit.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

Health data from Apple Health, Health Connect and Fitbit

Data read from Apple HealthKit, Android Health Connect or the Google Health API is used only to provide health features in this app to you, your linked family and your care team. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with data brokers. We only read this data; we do not write to your health apps. You can turn off access at any time in Apple Health, Health Connect, or by disconnecting Fitbit in the app's More screen.

Your consent and your rights

You give consent when you sign up. You can:

  • Withdraw consent and have your data erased in the app from More → Profile & settings → Withdraw consent. A coordinator verifies the request, then your account and member data are permanently deleted.
  • See or correct your data in the app, or ask us for a copy.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you are unable to, by contacting us.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer (below) and, if unresolved, to the Data Protection Board of India.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. After a verified erasure request we delete it, except a minimal record that consent was withdrawn, which we must keep to show we acted on your request. [retention period for clinical records, pending legal review]

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with access rules that limit each person to the members they are linked or assigned to. Uploaded documents are private and opened only through short-lived links. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Children

The app is meant for adults. It is not designed for children under 18.
